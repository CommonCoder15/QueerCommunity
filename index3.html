Sexual Orientation


Monosexual

An umbrella category for anyone attracted to only one gender (e.g., straight, gay, lesbian).

Heterosexual

Experiencing attraction exclusively to individuals of a different binary gender.

Homosexual

Experiencing attraction exclusively to individuals of the same sex or gender.

Gay

Experiencing attraction to individuals of the same gender (most commonly used by men).

Lesbian

A woman or non-binary person who experiences attraction exclusively other women or feminine-aligned people.


Multisexual

An umbrella category for anyone attracted to multiple genders (e.g., bisexual, pansexual, polysexual).

Bisexual

Experiencing attraction to two or more gender identities.

Pansexual

Experiencing attraction to people regardless of their gender identity; often described as "gender-blind" attraction.

Polysexual

Experiencing attraction to multiple, but not necessarily all, gender identities.

Omnisexual

Experiencing attraction to people of all genders, while actively recognizing and acknowledging their gender identity as part of the attraction.

Spectrasexual

Experiencing attraction to a specific, varied spectrum of multiple gender identities.


Asexual Spectrum

Experiencing little to no sexual attraction to anyone, or only under specific conditions.

Asexual

Experiencing little to no sexual attraction to anyone, regardless of romantic feelings.

Demisexual

Experiencing sexual attraction only after developing a deep, meaningful emotional bond with someone.

Gray-asexual

Falling in the gray area between asexual and sexual; experiencing sexual attraction rarely, weakly, or only under specific conditions.


Aromantic Spectrum

Experiencing little to no attraction to anyone, or only under specific conditions.

Aromantic

Experiencing little to no romantic attraction to anyone, regardless of physical attraction.

Biromantic

Experiencing romantic attraction to two or more gender identities, regardless of physical desire.

Demiromantic

Experiencing romantic attraction only after developing a deep, meaningful emotional bond with someone.

Gray-aromantic

Falling in the gray area between aromantic and romantic; experiencing romantic attraction rarely or weakly.


Allosexual

Experiencing sexual attraction to other people (the opposite of asexual).

Sapiosexual

Experiencing attraction rooted primarily in a person’s intelligence or mind rather than their physical appearance.

Abrosexual

Having a sexual orientation that is fluid, constantly changing, or unfixed over time.

Gynosexual

(Also spelled “gynesexual”)

Experiencing attraction specifically toward female anatomy, women, or feminine traits.

Androsexual

Experiencing attraction specifically toward masculinity, male characteristics, or masculine traits, regardless of the person’s gender identity.

Autosexual

Experiencing sexual attraction primarily or exclusively toward yourself.


Ceterosexual

Experiencing attraction exclusively toward non-binary or genderqueer individuals.


Finsexual

Experiencing attraction specifically toward women, femininity, or feminine traits.


Demirose

An umbrella term for someone who falls on both the asexual and aromantic spectrums (such as demisexual and demiromantic).


Demirofaint

Only experiencing attraction after building a deep emotional connection, but losing it once that bond is broken.
