Disclaimer

Gender and sexuality exist on a vast, multi-dimensional spectrum. If you don’t feel completely reflected by any of the terms listed, you are not alone and your experience is valid.


Queer

A broad, proud umbrella term for any orientation or identity that falls outside conventional societal norms.


Gender Expression


Feminine

Exhibiting clothing styles, grooming habits, and manners traditionally associated with women in a given culture.


Masculine

Exhibiting clothing styles, grooming habits, and manners traditionally associated with men in a given culture.


Femboy

A casual term for a boy or man (typically cisgender) who adopts traditionally feminine interests, clothing, and/or behavior.


A casual term for a girl or woman (typically cisgender) who adopts traditionally masculine interests, clothing, and/or behavior.


Androgynous (expression)

Displaying a combination of traditionally masculine and feminine visual traits, or an entirely gender-neutral appearance.


Gender-conforming

Presenting yourself in a way that matches what society traditionally expects for your gender identity.


Gender-nonconforming

Presenting yourself in ways that directly challenge or step outside societal expectations for your gender.

Drag Queen

An individual who adopts a highly exaggerated feminine persona, performance style, and dress for entertainment.

Drag King

An individual who adopts a highly exaggerated masculine persona, performance style, and dress for entertainment.

Butch

A term, often rooted in lesbian culture, for a style and demeanor that is traditionally masculine or rugged.

Femme

A term, often used alongside butch, for a style and presentation that is highly feminine.

Futch

A blend of butch and femme presentation styles, leaning comfortably in the middle.

Stem

A presentation style common in queer communities that blends stylistic elements of both "stud" (masculine) and "femme" presentation.
