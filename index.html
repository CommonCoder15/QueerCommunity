Disclaimer

Gender and sexuality exist on a vast, multi-dimensional spectrum. If you don’t feel completely reflected by any of the terms listed, you are not alone and your experience is valid.


Queer

A broad, proud umbrella term for any orientation or identity that falls outside conventional societal norms.


Gender Identities

Cisgender

Identifying with the exact gender and sex you were assigned at birth.


Transgender

An umbrella term for people whose internal gender identity differs from the sex they were assigned at birth. (Includes most non-binary, gender-fluid, and agender individuals, as well as binary trans men and trans women.)


Non-binary

An umbrella term for any gender that does not fit strictly into the categories of male or female.

Genderqueer

A term for identities that fall outside, reject, or cross the traditional male/female binary.

Gender-fluid

Having a gender identity that is not fixed and shifts or fluctuates over time.

Agender

Experiencing a complete absence of gender, or identifying as having a neutral gender.

Agender

Experiencing two distinct gender identities, either at the same time or alternating between them.

Neutrois

Identifying with a completely neutral or null gender that feels entirely distinct from male or female.

Trigender

Experiencing three distinct gender identities, either simultaneously or shifting between them.

Aporagender

Having a strong, definite sense of gender that is entirely non-binary and unrelated to maleness or femaleness.

Maverique

A specific non-binary identity characterized by a strong, independent sense of gender that is not male, female, or neutral.

Aliagender

Experiencing a gender identity that is entirely distinct from "male" or "female" categories.


Multigender

An umbrella term for anyone who experiences more than one gender identity.

Pangender

Experiencing a vast, multi-faceted gender identity that encompasses many or all genders at once.

Polygender

Experiencing multiple distinct gender identities simultaneously or over time.

Omnigender

Experiencing all possible gender identities within your cultural or personal framework.

Ambigender

Identifying as two genders simultaneously, with neither side dominating the other.


Demigender

An umbrella term for anyone who feels a partial connection to a specific gender identity.

Demiboy

Feeling a partial, but not complete, connection to being a boy or man.

Demigirl

Feeling a partial, but not complete, connection to being a girl or woman.

Deminonbinary

Feeling a partial, but not complete, connection to being non-binary.


Two-Spirit

A sacred, culturally specific umbrella term used by some Indigenous North Americans to describe a distinct blend of gender and spiritual identity.

Third Gender

A concept in many cultures recognizing a gender status that is neither male nor female.

Androgynous

Feeling your internal gender identity is a blend of both masculine and feminine traits.
